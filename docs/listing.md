# Listing (draft, from the tested 0.1.0 build)

Title: Horizon Strike
Tagline: Hunt Horizon Zero Dawn's machines across its real world with Counter-Strike 2 guns, kill money and the buy wheel.

## Description (Markdown)

Walk out of Mother's Heart into **Horizon Zero Dawn's own world** and hunt its machines with **Counter-Strike 2's arsenal**.

**What's in it**
- **Horizon's world, read from your copy of Horizon Zero Dawn Complete Edition:** the real terrain of all 340 main-world tiles, rocks, ruins, Nora huts and campfires on their original places, trees and undergrowth placed from the game's own density and snow maps, Horizon's exploration and combat music.
- **Horizon's machines:** Watchers, Striders and Grazers with their real models, skeletons and textures, standing at their real sites. Watchers patrol and go suspicious → alert → attack; herds graze and flee. Shoot the Watcher's eye or a Grazer's or Strider's blaze canister for weak-spot damage. Sites of other machines are filled with these three.
- **Counter-Strike 2's guns, read from your CS2 install:** real first-person models with CS2's own viewmodel animations, sounds, damage, fire rate, recoil, clip sizes and prices: knife, Glock, P250, Desert Eagle, MP9, UMP-45, Galil AR, AK-47, M4A1-S, AWP, Nova, HE grenade, Molotov and Kevlar.
- **CS economy:** start with knife, Glock and $800; every machine kill pays the CS2 kill award of your weapon class × the machine's bounty (max $16,000). Press **B** for the buy wheel (not in combat).
- **Death:** you respawn at the last campfire you visited with knife and Glock; other weapons are lost, money stays.

**First start:** the mashup converts your own game files into a local cache (about 1-2 minutes until you can play, ~0.8 GB). The rest of the world converts in the background as you approach it; the cache limit (default 4 GB) can be changed in the Esc menu. Nothing from either game is included in the download.

**Needs:** Counter-Strike 2 and Horizon Zero Dawn Complete Edition (Steam, original 2020 PC version) installed. If Horizon isn't found, the game tells you on screen. Counter-Strike 2 is never started or modified – only its files are read.

**Controls:** WASD move, Shift walk, Ctrl crouch, Space jump, mouse aim/fire, R reload, 1-4 weapons, B buy wheel, F inspect, Esc menu.

**Version 0.1 limits:** solo only; machines move with our own procedural animation on their real skeletons (Horizon's animation format can't be read); no people, quests or loot; no Frozen Wilds area.
